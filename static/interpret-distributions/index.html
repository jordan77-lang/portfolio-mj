How to interpret a frequency distribution

Greetings, future Head Chicken Checker!

To understand if something unusual is happening with our chickens, we first need to know what "normal" looks like. That is where frequency distributions come in.

A frequency distribution shows us patterns in our data. It reveals how values are spread out and where they tend to cluster. By learning to describe these patterns, you will be able to spot when something is typical and when something might be worth investigating.

In this training, you will learn how to interpret a frequency distribution by identifying its peaks and describing its shape.
