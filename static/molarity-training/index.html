Molarity to Mass Conversion

Scientists often report chemical concentrations in molarity (mol/L), which describes the number of moles of a substance per liter of solution. However, health organizations and regulatory agencies commonly express concentration in mass per volume, such as milligrams per liter (mg/L).

To compare laboratory data with safety guidelines, scientists must convert concentrations from molarity to mass per volume.

In this training, you will learn how to convert the molarity of a solute in a solution to its mass per volume of solution.
