## Overview

An educational Arizona State Retirement System (ASRS) pension estimator. Members walk through a guided wizard, then use live what-if controls and charts to explore retirement age, service, and pay tradeoffs.

## Source

- Repository: [github.com/jordan77-lang/ASRS-Estimator](https://github.com/jordan77-lang/ASRS-Estimator)

## Important

This is an educational planning tool, not an official ASRS benefit quote. Confirm figures in [myASRS](https://secure.azasrs.gov/) / [AzASRS.gov](https://www.azasrs.gov/).

## Features

- Guided estimation wizard
- Interactive what-if controls
- Vega-Lite visualizations for tradeoff exploration
- Unit-tested calculation engine
