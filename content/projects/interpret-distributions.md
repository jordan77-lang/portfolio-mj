Interactive training tool that teaches students how to interpret frequency distributions by identifying peaks (modality) and describing shape (symmetry) through a chicken-themed mission-based format with guided practice and immediate feedback.

## Key Features

- Step-by-step guided instruction with visual aids
- Interactive practice problems with real-time feedback
- Mission-based training format for student engagement
- Browser-based tool requiring no installation
- Easy integration into Canvas or other LMS platforms
