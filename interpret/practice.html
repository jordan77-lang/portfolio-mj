Complete solution

Question 1: Number of peaks

Answer: The distribution has a single peak.

Looking at the distribution, there is one clear high region at the 200-225 mL range where the bar is tallest (10 chickens). The bars on either side are shorter, creating a single peak pattern (unimodal distribution).

Question 2: Shape

Answer: The distribution has an asymmetrical shape.

If you imagine a vertical line through the peak (200-225 mL), the two sides do not perfectly mirror each other. The right side extends slightly further (with bars at 250-275 and 275-300) compared to the left side, making this distribution slightly asymmetrical (right-skewed).
